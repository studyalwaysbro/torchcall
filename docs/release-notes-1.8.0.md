**Busy ranges in fewer presses, and a way to let a quiet prospect go.**

A busy level range now splits into four slices instead of two when a search comes back full, and into single levels once it is narrow. Still one press per search, just fewer presses spent proving a range is crowded. And when the server caps the count, the note now says "at least 50 online" instead of repeating the cap back as if it were the truth.

Answered prospects used to pin to the top of the Prospects tab forever. Now the pin lasts three days. After that the row moves to the Contacted tab with nothing lost: Reply still works from there, and the record that stops anyone being asked twice stays. The Skip button now also shows on someone you invited who answered but never accepted, so a stale row can finally be let go. Skips older than two months are tidied away; the name stays remembered for good.

Offline checks: 1,244 tests and a 750,000 step soak, all green, and the design went through a council review first. The four way split and the three day pin have not been played on the live realm yet, so if a search or the list looks wrong, say so in the addons channel.

**How to update:** on CurseForge, hit Update. With Yeeterson Forge it arrives by itself. By hand: download the zip below, close the game fully, replace the `Torchcall` folder in `_forever_/Interface/AddOns/`. Your prospects, stats and settings are kept.
