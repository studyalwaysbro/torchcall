**Torchcall 1.9.1 fixes three invite problems.**

The guild-invite copy box could keep the keyboard after it closed, so the next Enter went nowhere instead of opening chat. Closing it now always returns the keyboard to the game.

Invites you prepare and cancel no longer use up your hourly or daily contact limit. An invite counts only when it actually reaches the player.

Invite counts after a player joins no longer double up. Torchcall also corrects the saved contact and funnel counts for blocked invites that 1.9.0 returns to the list.

**How to update:** on CurseForge, hit Update. With Yeeterson Forge it arrives by itself. By hand: download the zip below, close the game fully, replace the `Torchcall` folder in `_forever_/Interface/AddOns/`. Your prospects, stats and settings are kept.
