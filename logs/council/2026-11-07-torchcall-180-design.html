Quorum transcript

Design review for Torchcall 1.8.0 (WoW Forever guild-recruiting addon, Lua). HARD INVARIANTS that must survive: every who search / whisper / invite / guild-chat line is sent inside a click or keypress handler; NO timers sending anything, NO auto-continue of who searches (council killed an auto-chain on 11-05; do not re-open that door); one press = one action; who answers cap at 50 results; 5s gap between who searches; on the live client the SendWho filter TABLE is blocked, so queries are text like '16-28 c-"Mage"' and live data confirms the class words ARE honored (whoMode=text, no fallback tripped). Current pain, verified in code and the user's live SavedVariables: (P1) when a who answer caps at 50 and the band spans levels lo<hi, onWhoResults splits it into TWO halves queued at the front; sweeping a dense 13-level band costs roughly 2x the presses because every interior node is a wasted capped search. PROPOSAL A: on a capped answer split the band into up to FOUR even slices (per-level when width<=4); a slice that still caps splits again; still one press per search, Continue button shows the queue count. (P2) prospects who ANSWERED pin to the top of the Prospects tab via answered(p) = replied and (status whispered, or status invited and not linked). Rows with status 'invited' show ONLY a Reply button: the Skip button is gated to status new|whispered, so an answered-then-invited prospect who never accepts is pinned FOREVER with no removal path; the user has 13 such stale rows aged 1-2 days. PROPOSAL B: (i) show Skip also on answered rows whose status is invited (ns.Skip already handles any status: sets status=skipped, keeps the contacted record so they are never re-whispered); (ii) answered older than 3 days stops pinning: answered() gains an age window, the row falls to the Contacted tab, data intact, Reply/Answers still work there because they key off inConversation(p) which is independent of answered(); (iii) the header 'Answered N' counts only actionable (fresh, unskipped) answers. PROPOSAL C: when a capped answer reports total==shown==50 the note says 'at least 50 online' instead of 'showing 50 of 50 online' (the server caps the total too). Find real bugs, broken assumptions, bad interactions with the fuzz invariant 'nothing sent outside a press', data-loss risks in B, and anything that quietly re-opens timer-driven sends. Dissent explicitly welcome; do not rubber-stamp. --json --html
